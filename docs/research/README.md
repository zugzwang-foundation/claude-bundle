# Research notes (BDL-3)

Status: DRAFT — research note, 2026-10-16.

Six tracks, each written and then re-checked by a second agent on 16 October 2026 from sources opened that day. This page is the synthesis: what matters, where the relay's plan is wrong, and what to change. Every figure below is taken from a track file, which carries the full citation. Where a track author wrote "the relay says" about something that was only in that track's brief, this page says "brief".

## The ten findings that matter most

1. **No product files chats automatically, and OpenAI has the request on file with no date.** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok offer manual containers, memory, search and pinning; OpenAI Support replied on 6 September 2026 that there was no timeline, and the extensions that "auto-sort" use keyword lists or send titles to a cloud LLM, none publishing an accuracy. [prior-art.md](prior-art.md); https://community.openai.com/t/id-like-chatgpt-to-automatically-organize-my-conversations-projects-based-on-the-actual-content-of-each-chat/1388895/2 (checked 16 Oct 2026).
2. **Every decision model speaks one wire format, and "none" is just another option in it.** Jev, Clef, pplx-decider, Laya, H2O-Lightning and Microsoft-Decision-1 all use TypeSafe's System One `state` plus `questions` shape with no "none of these" primitive, so Bundle's native request maps losslessly onto a single `choice` question with `none` appended. [decision-models.md](decision-models.md); https://docs.typesafe.ai/api (checked 16 Oct 2026).
3. **Clef-flash is a middling comparator and no open decision model fits a browser.** On the Decision Index 0.3.2 (15 October 2026, 168 models) Clef-flash scores 47.61 (rank 63) while pplx-decider v1.1 scores 62.75 (rank 6) and vLLM-SR's d3 66.26 (rank 1), both Apache-2.0 and not gated, and the smallest Apache-2.0 decision model (Laya, 322M) is eight times the 40 MB budget at int8. [decision-models.md](decision-models.md); https://huggingface.co/spaces/multimodalart/jev-decision-index/resolve/main/data/v03.json (checked 16 Oct 2026).
4. **all-MiniLM-L6-v2 is the student base, and nothing multilingual fits 40 MB.** It is Apache-2.0, not gated, 22.7M parameters and 23 MB at int8 with official ONNX files, while every multilingual transformer found is 98–269 MB because the vocabulary alone is 90–250M parameters. [student-and-method.md](student-and-method.md); https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (checked 16 Oct 2026).
5. **"None" must be computed from the candidate scores, and out-of-scope is where small models fall over.** Max-softmax thresholding on a well-trained classifier matches specialised open-set methods, CLINC150 shows in-scope accuracy says little about out-of-scope recall, and Cloudflare's own table has Clef-flash at 66.77 macro-F1 on CLINC150+OOS against Clef's 97.43. [student-and-method.md](student-and-method.md), [decision-models.md](decision-models.md); https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.06207 and https://blog.cloudflare.com/clef-decision-models (checked 16 Oct 2026).
6. **WASM beats WebGPU for an int8 encoder.** Measured on this Mac in Chrome 155, MiniLM-L6 q8 takes 37 ms median on single-thread WASM, 14 ms with four threads and 53 ms on WebGPU; the fp32 file that makes WebGPU fast (8.7 ms) is 90 MB; under 4× CPU throttling, the phone stand-in, it takes 65–116 ms. [browser-and-web.md](browser-and-web.md), source 43 (local measurement, 16 Oct 2026).
7. **The relay's web budgets are wrong by construction.** Transformers.js in a worker is 154 KB gzip and React 67 KB, 221 KB before a line of our code, and the download must count a 0.7 MB tokenizer and 3.9–6.6 MB of WASM runtime, so a MiniLM-class site is 28–31 MB and a 33M-class one 39–41 MB. [browser-and-web.md](browser-and-web.md); https://registry.npmjs.org/ and `npm pack` inspection (checked 16 Oct 2026).
8. **The teacher is Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, and licences exclude the obvious alternatives.** It is Apache-2.0, not gated, a MoE with 3B active and about 102 tok/s single-stream on a Spark, whereas Gemma 3's Terms make any student a "Model Derivative", Llama 4 requires "Built with Llama" naming for models trained on its outputs and Persona Hub is CC BY-NC-SA; Gemma 4 (Apache-2.0) and Nemotron-Personas (CC BY 4.0) are the usable substitutes. [teacher-and-data.md](teacher-and-data.md); https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B and https://ai.google.dev/gemma/terms (checked 16 Oct 2026).
9. **The Spark is a batch machine with a stability problem.** Decode is bound by its 273 GB/s bus (a dense 27B at NVFP4 tops out near 14 tok/s per stream), only batching makes it a data factory (320–480 tok/s on 24–30B models, about 9–14M output tokens a night), and eight forum threads report hard power-offs or thermal stops under sustained load. [dgx-spark.md](dgx-spark.md); https://huggingface.co/blog/exolabs/the-dgx-spark-handbook (checked 16 Oct 2026).
10. **Bundle is email foldering re-done for chats, and that literature already has the exam design.** Bekkerman, McCallum and Huang trained on the first N messages and tested on the next N, ignoring items whose folder did not yet exist, and Gmail Priority Inbox cut error from 38% to 31% with per-user thresholds rather than better models. [prior-art.md](prior-art.md); https://people.cs.umass.edu/~mccallum/papers/foldering-tr05.pdf (checked 16 Oct 2026).

## Where the research says the relay's plan is wrong

| # | Track | The relay says | The evidence says |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | R4 | W5: the sorter "uses WebGPU where available and WASM otherwise" | int8 on WebGPU is slower than WASM (53 vs 37 ms), and the fp16/fp32 files that make WebGPU fast are 45/90 MB. WASM everywhere; WebGPU only as a tech-view experiment. |
| 2 | R4 | W5 budget: "JavaScript under 200 KB gzipped, before the model" | The libraries alone are 221 KB gzip (185 KB brotli); W5's own build today is 72 KB UI plus 155 KB worker. Restate as two numbers or accept about 300 KB. |
| 3 | R4 | W5 budget: "model download under 40 MB" | Honest only if it counts the tokenizer (0.7 MB) and WASM runtime (3.9–6.6 MB compressed). MiniLM class 28–31 MB passes; bge-small class 39–41 MB fails unless the host serves brotli. |
| 4 | R4 | W5 budget: "under 50 ms per decision on this Mac in Chrome" | Holds for a 6-layer 22M model in WASM (37 ms); fails for a 12-layer 33M model (71 ms) and for anything on the phone proxy (65–116 ms). Keep the spec's "well under a second on a phone" as the hard line; 50 ms is a Mac-only target. |
| 5 | R4 | W5: "the only third-party request is the model download" | Transformers.js fetches its WASM from jsdelivr and refuses local models until `env` is overridden. With `wasmPaths`, `allowLocalModels` and `allowRemoteModels` set, the site makes no third-party request at all; Playwright should assert that. |
| 6 | R2 | W6: PyTorch and Transformers.js must match at "cosine at least 0.999" after int8 export | 0.999 is the right bar for fp32 against fp32; int8 misses it and should be judged by exam effect. W3's dry run today measured a minimum cosine of 0.9944 at int8 (1.0 at fp32). |
| 7 | R2 | D6 and W6: cut-offs giving 95% and 80% precision, set by calibration on practice | The toy practice split is about 144 chats; a 95% cut-off set on it has error bars wider than the target. Temperature only at toy scale; the targets are testable only on the Spark run. |
| 8 | R2 | R2: "multilingual reach" alongside the 40 MB budget | No multilingual transformer under 98 MB exists; only a Matryoshka-truncated static model (about 27 MB, derived, quality unknown) does both. v0 is English-only; multilingual is a product decision for Hrishikesh. |
| 9 | R2 | Brief: an "explicit none" class, with CLINC150 as support for training one | CLINC150 does not favour a trained none class over a threshold (BERT's out-of-scope recall was higher with the threshold). None must be a function of top score, margin and candidate count, not a point in space. |
| 10 | R1 | D11: the open comparator is "Clef-flash (open)" | Clef-flash is rank 63 of 168 (47.61); d3-flash scores 55.79 at the same size, d3-mini 52.61 at 4B and pplx-decider v1.1 62.75. Add one. None of them runs on the 24 GB Mac in BF16. |
| 11 | R1 | Decision API v0: `none` appears only as a key in `probabilities` | Every System One engine treats none as one more option, so `none` must be a reserved candidate id the schema rejects; Jev, Clef and pplx-decider stop at 255 options, so the adapter must pre-select by nearest candidates. |
| 12 | R3 | W3: "Check mlx-lm... If it won't run, check for Ollama or llama.cpp" | mlx-lm's server documents neither `seed` nor JSON mode; llama-server documents both plus parallel slots. Reverse the order (W3 did: mlx_lm was absent and llama-server is running). |
| 13 | R3, R6 | D10: the Spark "runs the teacher and the real training" overnight | A dense teacher is capped near 14 tok/s per stream by the memory bus; the loop needs a ~3B-active MoE plus batching. R3 budgets 15M tokens at about 120 tokens a chat; R6 assumes 400 tokens a chat; the pilot must measure which. |
| 14 | R6 | R6: "running vLLM, SGLang or Ollama, fine-tuning tooling" (the brief named Unsloth) | Bulk generation is batched vLLM; Ollama is for convenience. Unsloth is a decoder tool shipped as a self-built arm64 image; the student is an encoder, so PyTorch in uv is enough. |
| 15 | R6 | D10 and R6: "running overnight" | Hard power-offs and thermal stops under sustained load are the recurring forum topic, plus a USB-C power-delivery firmware fault. Every long job must be resumable under systemd; the first night is a stability test. |
| 16 | R6 | D9: "the same code runs on the Mac and on the DGX Spark" | A plain `uv sync` on aarch64 installs CPU-only torch. The pyproject needs an explicit `pytorch-cu130` index routed by platform, written now, not at BDL-4. |
| 17 | R5 | SPEC: projects help "the people who set them up by hand; most people never do" | No source measures this. Soften to "need to be set up by hand" until there is evidence. |
| 18 | R5 | D7: lessons, practice and exam "split by person" | Necessary, not sufficient. Candidates must be the bundles that existed when the chat arrived; a chat whose bundle does not exist yet is "none"; report accuracy against history length. |
| 19 | R5 | W5 is "the biggest workstream" | OpenAI's Health nudge (January 2026), Space (September 2026) and the open support ticket say a closed version can ship at any time. The durable differentiator is the open model, data and exam; weight the scoreboard and release over demo polish. |
| 20 | R5 | R5: "multi-session memory" listed as prior art for the task | MSC, LoCoMo and LongMemEval test recall, not grouping, and LoCoMo is CC BY-NC. Never cite them as comparators; use CLINC150-style OOS and DSTC11 clustering metrics instead. |
| 21 | R4 | Rule 9: prefer releases "at least 14 days old" | Vite patched five release lines on 6 October 2026 with dev-server fixes, so pinning 8.3.3 at ten days is the sensible exception. TypeScript 7.0 is npm's latest, but Vite's own template stays on 6.0.x. |

What held: the four "checked facts" on Jev, Clef, Strands and Anthropic (R1); D5's small encoder student, since no open decision model comes near the budget (R1, R2); D2's fixed cut-offs for the demo (R5); B1 as the right baseline (R5); the choice not to be a browser extension (R5); D3's synthetic-only data, which the licence findings make unavoidable (R3, R5).

## Recommended changes

1. **Keep** Bundle's native v0 API as primary. **Add** a lossless System One `choice` adapter in both directions (TypeScript and Python), reserve the candidate id `none`, cap the adapter at 254 candidates plus `none` by nearest-candidate pre-selection, and store exam items in both forms so anyone can score the exam with any System One engine. (R1)
2. **Change** D11: keep Clef-flash, add pplx-decider v1.1 (or d3-flash; d3-mini if memory is short) as a second open comparator on the Spark, all Apache-2.0 and not gated. Re-pull the Decision Index data file before the list is fixed; it is one person's weekly snapshot. (R1)
3. **Keep** all-MiniLM-L6-v2 as the v0 base, with mxbai-embed-xsmall-v1 (same family, 4,096-token context, 24.4 MB) as challenger on the board. **Decide** how a chat is cut to MiniLM's 256 word pieces; that choice will move the exam more than the encoder does. (R2, R4)
4. **Change** the export path: R2 recommends `optimum-cli export onnx`, but W3 found optimum-onnx 0.1.0 pins transformers<4.58 and cannot coexist with sentence-transformers 6.1.0. Use `torch.onnx.export` plus `quantize_dynamic` (S8S8, per-channel), as W3 now does. Gate fp32 at 0.999; report int8's gap and its exam effect. (R2, W3)
5. **Change** "none" to a learned logit from top score, margin and candidate count, trained on the teacher's "belongs nowhere" chats and temperature-scaled on practice. **Drop** any fixed none vector and any `noul`-per-candidate design. (R2, R1)
6. **Change** W6's calibration at toy scale to temperature only, with bootstrap intervals and a "toy scale" label; isotonic and conformal wait for thousands of practice chats on the Spark. (R2)
7. **Change** W5: `device: 'wasm'` everywhere; `env.allowLocalModels = true`, `env.allowRemoteModels = false`, `wasmPaths = '/ort/'` with the ORT binaries copied into `web/public/ort/` at build; a `?device=webgpu` switch only in the tech view; a Playwright assertion that no request leaves the origin. (R4)
8. **Change** the budgets: UI bundle under 120 KB gzip and worker under 170 KB gzip (or one number near 300 KB); download as model plus tokenizer plus runtime, under 32 MB for the MiniLM class; latency as median and p90 over 40 decisions on this Mac, plus the same under 4× CPU throttle until a real phone is measured. (R4)
9. **Keep** the pins R4 names: vite 8.3.3, react 19.3.0, typescript 6.0.3, biome 2.5.15, vitest 5.0.3, playwright 1.63.0, @huggingface/transformers 4.3.0, onnxruntime 1.30.0, sentence-transformers 6.1.0. W5 scaffolded on vite 8.3.2 and typescript 7.0.2; if its build stays green, record the TypeScript exception in DECISIONS.md rather than churn. (R4, R2)
10. **Choose** Qwen3.6-35B-A3B through `nvidia/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-NVFP4` (Apache-2.0, not gated) as the Spark teacher, thinking off, `response_format` json_schema and the card's non-thinking sampling; Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507 (plain attention, best checked writing scores) as fallback A and gpt-oss-120b as fallback B; Gemma 4 26B-A4B-it for one person in five so the student does not learn one model's tics. **Drop** Gemma 3, Llama and Persona Hub on licence. (R3)
11. **Resolve** the container tag on day one of BDL-4: R3 pins `nvcr.io/nvidia/vllm:26.04-py3` (vLLM 0.19.0, the Qwen3.6 card's minimum, with a published Spark measurement); R6 pins `26.05-py3` (the current playbook); the playbook's own Qwen3.6 recipe uses upstream `vllm/vllm-openai`. `docker pull` the candidate, run ten minutes at 16 concurrent requests, and switch teacher if aggregate decode is under 200 tok/s. Record that the NGC container is under NVIDIA's EULA, not an open licence. (R3, R6)
12. **Add** to the pipeline now: difficulty tags by quota, verbalised-sampling openers (ask for five with probabilities, keep one at random), MinHash then embedding dedup, a per-run diversity report that fails on regression, decontamination of all splits against the demo library, and the exam's SHA-256 with prompt version and teacher hash in every scoreboard run. (R3)
13. **Change** `tasks` and `exam`: build each decision from the person's state at that moment in time order, label a chat whose bundle does not yet exist as "none", and report accuracy against the number of chats the person already has. **Add** ACC, NMI and ARI as secondary metrics so the DSTC11 community can read the board, and expect roughly a 14-point gain over the untrained baseline on that task, not more. (R5)
14. **Add** a deliberately written out-of-scope set, including near-misses (a running question that is not about the Running bundle), and report precision and recall for "none" above accuracy. (R5, R2, R1)
15. **Write** the Spark plan as R6's checklist: Ethernet, the supplied adapter, headless suspend masked, Tailscale Personal with `tailscale set --ssh` (needs Hrishikesh's account), the explicit `pytorch-cu130` uv index, batched vLLM at `--gpu-memory-utilization 0.7`, a resumable generator under systemd `Restart=on-failure`, and a first night that is a stability test. **Drop** Unsloth and Ollama from the plan. Buy the 128 GB, 4 TB unit, not the 64 GB one. (R6)
16. **Change** the README and tech view to say what Bundle is: email foldering re-done for chats, with open-intent discovery and out-of-scope detection, on device; cite the lineage; never claim to compete on memory benchmarks; date every product comparison. **Soften** "most people never do". (R5)
17. **Reweight** the rest of today and BDL-4 towards the scoreboard, the sealed exam and a releasable model and dataset, ahead of demo polish, because the product gap can close from the platform side at any time. (R5)
18. **Record** in docs/research/licences.md: Liquid d1 (LFM1.0 revenue cap) and Bekko (licence unsettled) excluded; LMSYS-Chat-1M (gated) and LoCoMo (CC BY-NC) named only to be ruled out; WildChat's two licence statements; the NGC container EULA; Persona Hub NC; Gemma 3 Terms. Every model recommended across the six tracks returns `gated: false` from the Hub API. (all)
19. **Open points for Hrishikesh:** English-only v0 or a 98–118 MB multilingual download; a Tailscale account; whether a one-off, never-published realism check of the exam against ShareChat titles fits the spirit of D3; the Spark's size if not yet bought; reading 20 pilot chats before the full Spark run; opening the demo on his phone and reading the tech-view latency. (R2, R6, R5, R3, R4)
